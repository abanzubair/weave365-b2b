# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Professional saree retailers, boutique owners, and reseller networks across India who curate collections for retail buyers. They prioritize fabric authenticity, design uniqueness, reliable wholesale pricing, and seamless order/inquiry workflows.

## Product Purpose
A premium B2B e-commerce platform that connects boutique owners and retailers directly with Varanasi weavers. Success means high conversion rates on bulk inquiries, seamless catalogs sharing on WhatsApp, and trusted verification of supplier-buyer partnerships.

## Brand Personality
- **Refined**: Luxury aesthetic inspired by traditional Indian weaving heritage.
- **Minimalist**: Clean layouts, intentional whitespace, and zero visual clutter.
- **Trustworthy**: Transparent operations, direct-from-weaver logistics, and verified partner networks.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered wholesale directories like KhatuJiWholesale.com that overload users with flashing colors, emojis as icons, and low-contrast text.
- Standard generic blue/purple SaaS dashboards that feel clinical and lack cultural craft connection.

## Design Principles
1. **Heritage through Modernity**: Blend traditional Banarasi motifs and premium typography (Cormorant Garamond, outfit) with a clean, high-contrast modern layout.
2. **Workflow Simplicity**: Ensure search, catalog navigation, and WhatsApp inquiry generation are frictionless and fast.
3. **No Decorative Clutter**: Every element must serve a functional purpose. Avoid nested cards, generic kickers, and arbitrary visual dividers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Follow WCAG 2.1 AA standards for color contrast, ensuring body text remains legible on all background tints.
- Fluid typography and responsive design optimized for standard mobile widths (down to 320px).
- Provide reduced-motion fallback options for custom transitions and animations.
